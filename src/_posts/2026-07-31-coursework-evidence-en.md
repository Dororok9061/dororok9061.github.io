Undergraduate coursework often combines reproducible source, older screenshots,
diagrams reconstructed from report values, and proposals that were never
implemented.

Placing these artifacts on one page must not give them the same evidence state.
A `Rerun` requires a new execution environment and result, an `Existing
Archive` must be identified as a historical record, and a `Portfolio Redraw`
is not a measured result.

The [coursework project page](/en/projects/coursework/) records these states
independently from the engineering domain.
