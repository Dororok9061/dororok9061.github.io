Using the ECG R-peak as a beat anchor makes it possible to align SCG and radar
waveforms across repeated cardiac cycles. A morphology peak or change point,
however, is not direct anatomical imaging.

AO and AC labels therefore remain **candidate timings**. The evidence behind
detector fusion and SQI selection should be recorded separately, together with
the validation boundary when independent echo, ICG, or PCG references are not
available.

The [radar project page](/en/projects/fmcw-radar/) stays within the scope
supported by public analysis source and hardware evidence.
