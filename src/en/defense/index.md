## Case studies

1. **Counter-UAS** — threat envelope, distributed layered architecture, threshold/objective values, and SIL/HIL/FMEA verification
2. **EW Jammer** — Spot/Barrage/DRFM trade-offs and an independently reproduced hypothetical S-band J/S calculation
3. **Reliability** — cleaned classroom records and reproduced MTBF, MTTR, and availability calculations

All values are limited to academic scenarios or classroom data from the source
reports. They must not be interpreted as real-system specifications, official
performance, or deployable operating procedures.
