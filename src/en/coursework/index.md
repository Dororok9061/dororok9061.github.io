## Domains

- **Digital Logic** — VHDL source and GHDL verification
- **Power Engineering** — transformer calculations and power-flow debugging
- **Control** — cascaded motor-control analysis
- **RF and Microwave** — passive-network design and existing Cadence archives
- **Sensors and Proposals** — sensing systems and an AESA-SAR research proposal

## Evidence policy

Newly executed results, existing archives, public redraws, source-derived
calculations, and proposals use different states. Work without Cadence, VNA, or
production-grid evidence is not presented as measured.
