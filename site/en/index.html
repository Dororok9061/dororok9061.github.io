Evidence Policy

Verified and unverified claims stay separate.

Source availability, execution, synthesis, and measurement are different states.

SOURCE AVAILABLE EXECUTED MEASURED BLOCKED
  1. Numbers remain traceable. Quantitative claims require a public CSV, log, waveform, photograph, report, or reproducibility script.
  2. Validation stages are not merged. RTL simulation, synthesis, PPA, and hardware measurement are recorded separately.
  3. Photographs have a limited role. They show implementation presence, not complete accuracy, performance, or clinical validity.
  4. Missing evidence is explicit. Not reported, Needs confirmation, and BLOCKED are never relabeled as PASS.
